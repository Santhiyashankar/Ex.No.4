# Explanation
Scenario
The manufacturing industry is seeking ways to reduce manual monitoring and increase efficiency through automation. The system integrates IoT devices and embedded controllers to automate equipment, monitor performance, and enable predictive maintenance. The goal is to streamline the production process, minimize downtime, and enhance energy efficiency.
Target Audience

Manufacturing companies in automotive, electronics, and food processing sectors.
Industries that can achieve significant productivity improvements via automation.

# Main Objectives

Improve production efficiency by 30%.
Minimize machinery downtime using predictive maintenance.
Enable real-time monitoring and remote control of manufacturing systems.
Reduce energy consumption by optimizing processes.


# Procedure
1. Comparative Analysis Prompt
Definition: Asking for a comparison between two or more concepts, highlighting similarities and differences.
Prompt:

"Compare traditional manual monitoring with the IoT-enabled automated system in terms of efficiency, downtime, and energy usage for a manufacturing plant."

# Generated Analysis:
ParameterTraditional Manual MonitoringIoT-Enabled Automated SystemEfficiencyLabor-intensive; prone to human error; inconsistent output.30% efficiency increase through continuous automated control.DowntimeReactive maintenance; frequent and prolonged downtimes.Predictive maintenance reduces unplanned downtime significantly.Energy UsageNo automated optimization; excess consumption common.Automated load balancing and demand-based switching cut energy waste.ScalabilityDifficult to scale without hiring additional personnel.Cloud-integrated and easily scalable across multiple plants.Data AccuracyManual logs susceptible to errors and delays.Real-time sensor data provides accurate, timestamped records.
Evaluation (Rubric Score: 9/10):

Relevance: 5/5 – Directly addresses the manufacturing automation scenario.
Depth: 4/5 – Covers key parameters; could include cost comparison for full marks.


2. Experiential Perspective Prompt
Definition: Prompts that ask for a response from the point of view of a specific role or stakeholder experiencing the subject first-hand.
Prompt:

"As a factory floor operator who has worked with both manual systems and the new IoT-enabled automation platform, describe your experience. How has it changed your daily routine, safety, and productivity?"

Generated Output:
"Before the IoT system, I had to walk the floor every hour to check machine temperatures and vibration levels manually. Alarms were often missed, and breakdowns caught us by surprise. Now, a dashboard on my tablet shows every machine's health in real time. When a motor starts overheating, I get an alert before it becomes a problem. Shift handovers are faster because I just share the live dashboard link. Productivity has noticeably improved and the job feels safer."
Evaluation (Rubric Score: 8/10):

Authenticity: 5/5 – First-person narrative is realistic and grounded.
Coverage: 3/5 – Could also include energy-saving observations from the operator's perspective.


3. Everyday Functioning Prompts
Definition: Prompts that relate a technology or concept to common, day-to-day activities to make it easier to understand and apply.
Prompt:

"Describe the role of IoT sensors and automated controllers in a manufacturing plant the same way a smartphone's battery management system works in everyday life."

# Generated Output:
Just as a smartphone continuously tracks battery level and automatically dims the screen, reduces background activity, or switches to low-power mode to extend battery life — the IoT sensors in a factory continuously monitor machine temperatures, vibration, and power draw. When demand is low, the system automatically scales back non-critical machinery (like a phone closing unused apps). When a sensor detects an abnormal reading, it triggers a maintenance alert (like a low-battery warning) — long before the machine actually fails.
Evaluation (Rubric Score: 9/10):

Clarity: 5/5 – Analogy is easy to understand for a non-technical audience.
Accuracy: 4/5 – Concept mapping is correct; technical depth is slightly simplified.


4. Universal Prompt Structures / Prompt Refinements

5. Prompt Size Limitations
Definition: Managing input or output size by breaking large prompts into smaller, manageable chunks to work within AI token limits and improve response quality.
Large Prompt (Problematic):

"Summarize the entire 5000-word technical specification for the IoT manufacturing automation system."

Chunked Prompt (Refined):

"Summarize the entire IoT manufacturing automation system in fewer than 40 words."

Generated Summary (< 40 words):
"A cloud-based IoT automation platform enabling real-time equipment monitoring, predictive maintenance, and energy optimization to improve manufacturing efficiency, reduce downtime, and enhance scalability across automotive, electronics, and food processing industries."
Further Chunking Example:
PromptOriginal"Give me 50 examples of AI applications in manufacturing."Adjusted"Give me 10 AI applications in manufacturing at a time. Start with predictive maintenance applications."
Evaluation (Rubric Score: 9/10):

Conciseness: 5/5 – Stays within 40 words and covers all key aspects.
Clarity: 4/5 – Slightly dense; could be split into two sentences for better readability.
